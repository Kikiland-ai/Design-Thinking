## Objective and why

A startup jury scores the business as hard as the build. The story is: **deep where it counts, broad by design.** The student route is real, deployed and deep. The trader and streamer prototypes show the same system re-targeted at very different sectors, where the remaining work is content rather than engineering. This session turns the hypotheses in [01 §8](../01-kiki-for-knowledge-workers.md#8-b2b-positioning-hypotheses-not-findings) into evidence and a decision. It turns [04](../04-judge-story.md) into a deck the team delivers twice within time.

Useful BB8 skills, if available in the session: `deep-research`, `product-strategy`, `deck-craft`, `minto-communication`.

## Deliverables

1. **Rubric mapping first (by 13:00).** Get the buildathon's official judging criteria and final format: minutes, live demo or video, the juror mix. Map every criterion to a slide and a demo moment. Report gaps to the hub at once: this can change the build.
2. **Lab clip (by 13:00).** Pick and trim the real MATLAB recording (live checks, Now/Next window) for the "Do" beat, because the hosted lab trial budget is used up. Source candidates: `Google AI Builder/brag-output/` and `KIKI-LAB-RECORDING-HANDOFF.md`.
3. **Evidence pack** (`06-gtm-evidence.md`), every figure with source and date:
   - SEBI's FY26 study of individual F&O traders: primary report, exact figures.
   - Onboarding cost and time to productivity in tool-heavy roles.
   - India's multilingual workforce and Indic-language internet use.
   - Engineering students from regional-medium schooling.
   - The regional-language creator economy.
4. **Beachhead decision support.** Score the options in [01 §8](../01-kiki-for-knowledge-workers.md#8-b2b-positioning-hypotheses-not-findings) on pain, budget, access, KiKi fit and speed to a pilot. With the student route as the working build, the strongest candidates are probably colleges and employers hiring regional-medium engineers. Recommend one; the team decides (D5).
5. **Business model:** per seat plus per-pack setup; unit economics from S3's measured cost per tutor session (E4).
6. **Positioning by category, with checked facts:** learning platforms, AI tutors, digital adoption platforms. Make no claim about a named competitor without a current source.
7. **Deck v1 by 19:00** following [04 §5](../04-judge-story.md#5-pitch-deck-skeleton-startup-format), with speaker notes; demo script with timings ([04 §4](../04-judge-story.md#4-demo-narrative-about-6-minutes-adjust-to-the-slot)).
8. **Juror simulation** at 19:00 and 01:00: one technical and one business juror against the rubric. Log every question the team can't answer yet.
