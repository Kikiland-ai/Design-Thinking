# KiKi for knowledge workers (Google Startup Buildathon)

v0.2 · 20 Nov 2026 · Status: **Scope agreed; owners to confirm at the 09:00 kickoff** · Build: 09:00 Fri → 09:00 Sat IST

KiKi widens from a bilingual learning app for Odia-medium engineering students into an **active multilingual learning companion for knowledge workers**, sold to organisations for onboarding and role transitions. Three personas span three kinds of knowledge work: an ECE student (laws), an F&O trader (odds) and a GTA 6 streamer (audiences). **The student route is the real, deep build; the trader and streamer are prototype videos in the same formats** ("deep where it counts, broad by design").

## The idea in five lines

1. People in a new role lose performance to the working language and unwritten know-how, not ability.
2. KiKi compiles an organisation's own material into lessons in each person's language (**Learn**), coaches inside the real tool (**Do**) and talks problems through on a decision graph (**Reflect**).
3. Evidence is split four ways: concept, words, expression, practice. A manager sees where the gap is.
4. Deep where it counts: the student journey is real, from narration-synced lesson to tutor mode to lab.
5. Broad by design: the formats (graphs, placards, cues, four-way evidence) don't know their sector. The trader and streamer prototypes show the same system re-targeted, and the business sells into one beachhead first.
